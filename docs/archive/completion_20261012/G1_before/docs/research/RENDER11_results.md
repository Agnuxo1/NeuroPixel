# Sustained device energy: original incomplete cohort

Actual RTX 3090 cumulative board counters were measured in three-second active blocks, with one-second idle observations before and after. All 77 registered output gates passed. The original execution preserves **100 of 105 blocks** and stopped when available host RAM fell below the unchanged 8 GiB floor.

The missing five blocks are the final technical block for the RGB retina workload. Operational recovery may measure only those missing keys and must retain all original rows, source hashes, inputs and limits. Until the complete cohort is admitted, no complete comparative energy effect is reported.

Raw counter values, call counts, elapsed times, idle rates and temperatures are retained in `results/research/RENDER11_energy/original_20261009A`. Gross values include board background consumption; baseline adjustment is an estimate, not a confidence interval or unique process attribution. CPU, RAM, PSU and whole-system wall energy are excluded. Partial measurements are evidence of actual measurement, not proof of an energy advantage.

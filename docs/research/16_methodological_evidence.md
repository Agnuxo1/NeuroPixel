# SCI-016: interpreting the native scanner and causal evidence

Review date: 2026-10-07 (UTC). Scope: SCI-016 only. This is a source and design review, written before this item's proposed controls execute. It records no new model performance, learned-state intervention, numerical rank result or mechanism discovery. Repository inspection uses commit `d819411f057a9e31f708f630f73f2773826a146e`. The literature search is focused, not systematic or a priority review.

## What the implementation supports

The [core](https://github.com/Agnuxo1/NeuroPixel/blob/d819411f057a9e31f708f630f73f2773826a146e/neuropixel/model.py#L70) applies the same affine state readout and effective token dictionary at every inspected cell. The [scanner](https://github.com/Agnuxo1/NeuroPixel/blob/d819411f057a9e31f708f630f73f2773826a146e/neuropixel/scanner.py#L17) then suppresses PAD with a finite logit, computes softmax, and retains the top token and its probability. This is a native readout visualization without a separately fitted diagnostic classifier. It is not a direct measurement of every state coordinate, a calibrated semantic confidence measure, or evidence of which intermediate features cause the answer.

The scanner's own introductory comment already limits it to an interpretive lens rather than a causal test. Its accompanying statement that the readout is trained only at the output cell is recipe-dependent: [school supervision](https://github.com/Agnuxo1/NeuroPixel/blob/d819411f057a9e31f708f630f73f2773826a146e/neuropixel/research/experiment.py#L300) applies cross-entropy to lens outputs at occupied input cells when enabled. Readable input identities can therefore be directly supervised. They are not independent evidence of an emergent reasoning mechanism. Core school lenses are collected before updates divisible by four; traced frames comprise the initial state and post-update states. Analyses must name the actual state index.

The [README](https://github.com/Agnuxo1/NeuroPixel/blob/d819411f057a9e31f708f630f73f2773826a146e/README.md#L92) describes interpretability by construction. The implementation establishes readout sharing; completeness and causal faithfulness require separate evidence. Appropriate wording is: **The scanner applies the model's learned readout throughout the grid. It visualizes token predictions under that projection, which can omit state information and does not alone explain the computation.** The [scan command](https://github.com/Agnuxo1/NeuroPixel/blob/d819411f057a9e31f708f630f73f2773826a146e/scripts/scan.py) saves selected-time token labels, not complete state and probability trajectories. Those historical JSONs alone cannot establish causal completeness.

## Five directly read sources and their limits

1. **Hewitt and Liang (2019), Designing and Interpreting Probes with Control Tasks.** [Original proceedings PDF](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1275.pdf), §§1–2 and §3.6; printed pp.2733–2735, 2739. They distinguish information recoverable by a trained probe from learning performed by that probe, using random word-type labels and selectivity controls. This motivates separating representational decoding from model behavior. NeuroPixel's fixed native scanner does not incur the identical extra-probe training confound, so their numerical probe results do not transfer to it. Nor does eliminating that confound establish causal faithfulness. Relevant full-text passages, not only the abstract, were read.

2. **Belrose et al. (2023), Eliciting Latent Predictions from Transformers with the Tuned Lens.** [Author preprint PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.08112), §§2–3, equations (3)/(8), and §4, especially §4.1. The paper contrasts direct intermediate unembedding with learned affine translators and separately evaluates causal fidelity through feature interventions and agreement with final-model effects. That separation is useful here: a lens's predictions and its faithfulness are different empirical questions. Their transformer findings do not validate NeuroPixel's spatial lens, and NeuroPixel is not implementing the learned translators. The served 25-page full text was read in relevant sections; no version suffix is inferred from retrieval.

3. **Elazar et al. (2021), Amnesic Probing: Behavioral Explanation with Amnesic Counterfactuals.** [Original TACL PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2021.tacl-1.10.pdf), §§2.1–2.3, printed pp.161–163, and §3.3. They remove linearly predictable information and measure downstream behavior, with controls for generic rank reduction and unintended removal of correlated information. Thus an intervention effect need not uniquely identify the intended semantic property. Their supervised property erasure differs from perturbing the null space of NeuroPixel's existing readout: the latter has no automatically assigned linguistic meaning. Relevant full text was read.

4. **Zhang and Nanda (2024 version), Towards Best Practices of Activation Patching in Language Models: Metrics and Methods.** [Original preprint PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.16042v2), dated 17 January 2024; §§2–3, §4 and §6. In their studied models, corruption choices and output metrics change localization results. They discuss distribution shift from noise, saturated probability metrics, and nonlinear joint-patching effects. This supports declaring intervention and metric choices before observing results and retaining full outputs. It does not imply that every perturbation is invalid, that a null effect proves non-use, or that a naturally sourced patch guarantees an on-distribution combined state. The relevant full-text passages were read through the unversioned PDF, whose header explicitly identifies v2.

5. **M. D. Lemmon (2024), Lectures on Linear Systems Theory.** [Author's academic notes](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~lemmon/courses/linear-systems/lecture-book/linsys-book-2024.pdf), chapter 4 §4, printed pp.182–183 (PDF pages 188–189, counting from one). For a discrete linear system with known inputs, temporal observability concerns the stacked maps C, CA, …, CA^(n−1), not C alone. An instantaneous null direction can become visible later. These are mathematical lecture notes, not a new empirical benchmark; the linear theorem does not certify observability or non-observability of this nonlinear NCA. The discrete-time derivation was read directly.

All five sources were accessed on 2026-10-07. Primary texts and author notes were preferred over summaries; no exhaustive literature coverage is claimed.

## Instantaneous readout, displayed output and temporal observability

For a cell state s, write the unmasked logits as D(Rs + b), where R is the learned 16-by-48 read matrix and D is the effective dictionary. Rank-nullity gives dim ker(R) ≥ 32 in the ambient state space. A perturbation in this kernel leaves these instantaneous logits unchanged in exact arithmetic; the bias cancels. PAD replacement, softmax and top-token display must be distinguished from this linear map. Numerical rank is tolerance-dependent and must be reported with the declared rule and singular values.

This dimensional fact neither characterizes the reachable learned-state manifold nor proves the projection is noninjective on that manifold. It does not identify a role, a memory, or a feature that the trained dynamics uses. Nor does it show that a known transition rule and an entire output sequence fail to reveal the hidden state. Temporal observability asks about successive compositions with the transition map, a stronger question. Conversely, assigned weights can keep a hidden direction invisible forever or couple it into later visible coordinates. Such constructions establish possibilities admitted by the architecture, not their occurrence in trained checkpoints.

The displayed top token and maximum probability lose information even relative to the full probability vector. Exchanging two nonwinning probabilities can leave that display unchanged. Separately, `lens_logits` has raw PAD logit zero because the effective dictionary's PAD row is zero; scanner and final answer paths replace PAD by −10000. These are different normalization conventions. A finite sentinel is not exclusion by negative infinity: it can win if all other logits are smaller. Standard softmax is invariant to shifting every logit together, but a fixed PAD coordinate prevents treating shifts of only the non-PAD logits as exactly invisible. Ordinary-scale and extreme-boundary examples must be labelled accordingly, without implying their frequency in learned models.

## Minimum controls within the agreed item

The agreed native control uses a four-coordinate state and a three-coordinate readout, with two assigned states that differ only in the unread coordinate. A specified coupling can make their next outputs differ, while identity dynamics supplies the negative control. Its conclusion is a counterexample to completeness of the instantaneous scanner, not a learned explanation. The probability-compression and PAD-policy controls test separate information losses and API conventions. The five authenticated learned matrices provide a rank-only check: no learned forward pass, null perturbation, behavioral effect or semantic attribution is included.

For these controls, preserve the assigned tensors, complete logits and probability vectors, exact intervention boundary, unchanged weights and inputs, and the identity comparison. Check equality of full instantaneous readouts rather than only displayed labels; check the distinct subsequent outputs only where the assigned coupling predicts them. Prespecify numerical tolerances and retain counterexamples or failures. Counts of constructed states or matrices are inventories, not independent performance replicates. No confidence interval or generalization estimate follows from them.

A future causal role explanation would additionally need a competent behavior to explain, an explicit semantic hypothesis, matched factual/counterfactual cases, and interventions that separate the claimed feature from generic corruption or correlated features. Baseline restoration and unchanged-input controls would be essential. Any deliberately off-manifold learned null perturbation would establish, at most, a local conditional intervention effect, not a role code. These are limits on the claim, not authorization for additional experiments in this item.

# Item 9 — Guide to interpreting the frozen final estimands

Prepared on 2026-10-07 during the study frozen at source [83fe135e301f76bc0c74e30c66bb18e067ca5959](https://github.com/Agnuxo1/NeuroPixel/commit/83fe135e301f76bc0c74e30c66bb18e067ca5959). No partial model outcomes were consulted. This guide explains the existing [protocol](09_protocol.md), [study plan](09_study_plan.json) and [analytical addendum](09_analytical_addendum.md); it adds no test, decision threshold or selection rule. Historical H1 remains closed.

## 1. Start with the quantity being estimated

The question is whether a trained system retrieves the correct filler from an explicit event–role query when two complete events compete. Read the complete declared panel before interpreting any favorable condition. Integrity checks, complete checkpoint coverage and independently recounted predictions are prerequisites to a performance conclusion; a passing software audit is not itself evidence of binding competence.

| Frozen quantity | What a favorable value supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| **Base macro agent/patient accuracy** | Correct noun-role retrieval, with equal weighting of the two events and noun roles, on sampled held-out bags | Competence on every scenario, all roles, all transformations or other grammars |
| Global and per-role accuracy | Which of the four queried roles succeed; whether an aggregate obscures uneven performance | Global accuracy alone cannot distinguish noun-role resolution from easier category discrimination |
| All-eight correctness | The fraction of scenarios for which all eight declared queries are correct | A universal guarantee; or an independent sample of eight trials per scenario |
| Mean cross-entropy | How much probability is assigned to the true answers, including penalties for confident errors | A monotonic substitute for accuracy, or a complete assessment of calibration |
| Base-and-variant joint correctness | Both members of a prescribed input pair are answered correctly | Identification of the model's internal representation or elimination of all possible shortcuts |
| Prediction equality/inequality on eligible pairs | Behavioral stability or responsiveness in the direction required by the transformation | Correctness without checking both answers against their gold targets |
| Paired NeuroPixel-minus-Transformer difference | Relative performance under the selected rates, fixed budget, data and architecture configurations | Universal architecture superiority, equal optimization opportunity or a causal account of an internal component |

The primary noun-role endpoint matters because plausible filler selection is easier than correct binding. The declared controls have analytical binding expectations of 0.25 for bag/category guessing and 0.5 for both role-only and event-aware category matching. Their global expectations are 0.375, 0.5 and 0.75, respectively. Compare absolute performance with these specified alternatives. Exceeding a control shows an advantage over that particular rule, not proof that every heuristic has been ruled out. Uniform control probabilities also do not determine an all-eight joint success probability without an additional assumption about how guesses are coupled; the study introduces no such assumption.

## 2. Separate correctness from trivial invariance

A predictor that always returns the same token has perfect prediction equality whenever an intervention should leave the answer unchanged, even when every answer is wrong. Conversely, producing different tokens after a relevant intervention does not imply either token is correct. The report must therefore put the joint-correct rate beside equality or inequality, with its eligible denominator.

For each paired base and variant, joint correctness is the indicator that **both predictions equal their respective targets**. Its denominator is the prescribed paired set, including pairs where the base answer is wrong. It is not the probability of variant success conditional on the base already being correct. The phrase “conditional bootstrap interval” elsewhere refers to fixed trained checkpoints, not to filtering out incorrect base responses.

The queried agent/patient swap changes the target for noun queries but leaves action/place targets unchanged. Combining those cases into one undifferentiated “change rate” would reverse the intended interpretation for half the queries. Other-event swaps, global event renaming together with the query, and layout permutation should preserve the answer. Query-event switching should change it because same-role fillers are distinct. The frozen analyzer retains these eligibility sets; an empty set has denominator zero and an undefined/null rate, not perfect performance.

**Post-launch interpretive clarification (2026-10-07, before final results were supplied to or inspected by this reviewer):** the frozen [renderer](https://github.com/Agnuxo1/NeuroPixel/blob/83fe135e301f76bc0c74e30c66bb18e067ca5959/neuropixel/research/complex_binding_data.py#L291) chooses the affected event separately for each query: the queried event for `swap_queried_agent_patient`, and the other event for `swap_other_agent_patient`. Each swap condition therefore combines four queries from each of two transformed fact grids within the same bag. Its all-eight score is the conjunction of eight **query-conditioned swap cases**, not eight answers about one fixed transformed scene. Label it accordingly in the final report. This changes neither saved targets nor arithmetic, pairing, bag-level resampling or decisions: the competence screen's all-eight threshold applies to **base**, whose eight queries share one fixed scene. The source and metric definitions remain frozen; no experiment or result prompted this clarification.

Successful pairs support responsiveness to the manipulated association while retaining correctness. They do not identify whether that behavior arises from tensor-product codes, another distributed representation, memorized subpatterns or another implementation. Smolensky's formal binding/unbinding results concern a specified representation, not every system that produces correct answers ([primary paper, §3.1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/Smolensky-90-AI-Tensor-Product-Variable-Binding-and-the-Representation-of-Symbolic-Structures-in-Connectionist-Systems.pdf)). HANS illustrates why targeted counterexamples can expose particular heuristics without certifying all reasoning mechanisms ([McCoy et al., 2019, §§2–3](https://aclanthology.org/P19-1334.pdf)).

## 3. Understand the deliberate query-switch duplication

Every bag has eight base queries. Switching the queried event maps each one to another base query on exactly the same scene. Consequently, the query-switch condition supplies no new unique input: it reorders the base set. Under consistent deterministic predictions, its marginal accuracies and all-eight correctness must match base by permutation. Matching these quantities is an integrity consequence, not an independent replication of successful generalization.

Its paired diagnostic still has a purpose. It asks whether both event-specific answers for a role are correct and whether the prediction changes appropriately when the selector changes. A system that gives the same answer for both events can be correct for one of their distinct targets while failing the pair. The joint rate reveals that failure even when marginal query-switch accuracy repeats base accuracy exactly.

The source-derived expectation is 48 nominal rows but 40 unique canvases per bag: 32 occur once and eight twice. Across 256 bags this predicts 12,288 rows and 10,240 unique canvases per checkpoint. The final audit must verify actual identities and duplicate agreement. Neither nominal rows nor unique canvases replace the **256 bag/scenario units** used for resampling.

## 4. Read the two kinds of uncertainty separately

**Across training realizations:** the primary comparison uses five paired differences, one for each seed 40–44. Each seed changes initialization and the private minibatch stream, plus NCA firing. Thus the interval describes five complete training realizations, not isolated initialization noise. Corresponding families share inputs; their internal computations and stochastic operations are not equivalent.

The reported mean difference uses a two-sided 95% t interval with df=4 and sample SD. Inspect all five differences, their range and any variability in sign. An interval above zero supports a positive mean difference under the stated sampling/model assumptions and recipe; one below zero supports the reverse. An interval crossing zero does not demonstrate equivalence. Five pairs provide limited information about the outcome distribution, and nominal coverage depends on the small-sample assumptions. The interval excludes uncertainty from choosing LR, generating another dataset or selecting another task. Do not append development pilots, drop a difficult seed or silently replace missing declared pairs.

**Across scenarios at fixed checkpoints:** the 2,000-resample percentile bootstrap draws whole bags, keeping their eight queries and six conditions together. The same index vectors are shared across checkpoints and conditions, preserving paired comparisons. This addresses variation over the sampled scenario distribution conditional on the trained models. It does not supply thousands of independent training runs or repair the limited five-run estimate.

If a binary metric is constant across all observed bags—for example, no scenario has all eight answers correct—the empirical percentile interval can collapse to [0,0] or [1,1]. This does not establish a population probability of exactly zero or one, or nominal coverage at the boundary. Show the observed numerator and denominator and interpret the interval as the prespecified bootstrap summary; do not introduce a replacement test or decision criterion after seeing the outcomes.

Raw query denominators per condition are 2,048 globally, 512 per role and 1,024 for binding; all-eight correctness has 256 scenarios. Some analyzer interval fields instead use per-bag means with denominator 256. Those are scenario contributions, not query counts. Keep their unit labels when rendering tables. The independently pinned analysis environment and exact saved resampling indices identify the actual arithmetic; differing training and analysis runtime versions are not a claim of bitwise cross-version equivalence.

Secondary intervals describe the complete declared diagnostics. Selecting only intervals that exclude zero would create a discovery claim for which no family-wide error-control procedure was declared. The number of conditions does not increase the number of independent training realizations.

## 5. Keep generalization and competence claims bounded

A held-out bag is a previously excluded complete set of four nouns, two verbs and two places. Its primitive tokens, event labels, role labels, rendering grammar and two-event template are already known. Smaller combinations may occur in training. The final design samples one assignment per bag, so it does not exhaust all assignments or layouts of even those held-out bags. The very large rendering space is a count of possibilities, not evidence that all were tested or that the task demands general reasoning.

SCAN distinguishes specific composition splits; COGS separates lexical/role generalization from structural and recursive extensions; CFQ explicitly controls atomic and compound distributions. Those distinctions justify naming the exact novelty of this split, rather than calling it unrestricted systematicity ([Lake and Baroni, 2018](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/lake18a/lake18a.pdf); [Kim and Linzen, 2020](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.731.pdf); [Keysers et al., 2020](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.09713.pdf)). CLEVR's diagnostic construction motivates checking explicit factors and shortcuts, but this symbolic grid does not test its visual-perception demands ([Johnson et al., 2017](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.06890.pdf)). Full reading limits are recorded in the [methodological evidence](09_methodological_evidence.md).

The predeclared competence screen requires all five NeuroPixel checkpoints to meet base binding ≥0.95, base all-eight ≥0.90 and binding ≥0.90 in every declared condition. Apply it to unrounded values and report each component. Meeting it supports competence within this constrained setting; an advantage over the Transformer is a separate comparison. Missing it leaves the screen unmet under this recipe and budget. Low probes or successful tiny-fixture memorization do not, by themselves, establish why a final result occurred or authorize another budget.

A defensible final conclusion names the complete inventory, fixed budget, absolute scores, paired uncertainty and which counterfactuals were answered correctly. It retains failures and distinguishes integrity evidence from performance. It does not infer natural-language parsing, anaphora, repeated-entity binding, recursion, more than two events, biological plausibility or external-domain generalization. The local final-access gate and authenticated archive support documented provenance; they are not external custody or replication. None of the possible outcomes reopens historical H1 or certifies scientific priority.

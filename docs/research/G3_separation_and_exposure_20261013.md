# G3: separation units, selector and test exposure (13 October 2026)

**Separation unit for new relational work.** The ordered relation-composition task now separates data by *structural family*: the class of a relation graph under any renaming of entities and any permutation of relation labels (`scripts/completion_g3_splits.py:family_id`). The canonical form is an exact invariant: on small graphs it agrees with brute-force isomorphism in every tested pair (`tests/test_completion_g3_splits.py`). Renamed variants are generated inside their family, so no family crosses splits.

**Registered development data.** `results/research/NEWREL20261013/` holds train (96 families, 288 renamed graphs, 2,304 examples), DEV (24 families, 576 examples) and a paired DEV-long panel at depth 4. Train and DEV share no family (`G3_newrel_family_audit.json`). TEST is deliberately absent: the designated evaluator will generate it with a committed seed, excluding the 120 listed families (`excluded_families.json`, SHA-256 `dee28f3b...`). The kit's own default generator, which groups only exact graph identities, shows no structural-family overlap at its default seed; its base and long TEST panels share graphs by design.

**Fixed selector.** Most DEV correct answers, then lowest DEV NLL, then earliest epoch; never TEST, never the best seed (`select_checkpoint`).

**Exposure and custody.** `results/research/COMPLETION20261012/G3_split_registry.json` logs every evaluation set in use, its unit, its custody and each TEST access with its receipt. Checked from receipts: the ten VIS07 models were sealed before the single official TEST scoring; later VIS07 diagnostics on that TEST (depth, GPU fidelity, replay) made no selection; VIS07, DEV04, GLOB05 and READ03 never scored the 264 original RoleTask compositions. No current evaluation set is under blind custody. Leakage review follows the taxonomy of Kapoor and Narayanan (2023). Open item: the exact-image uniqueness and disjointness of the UCI digits splits are asserted by the manuscript without a retained receipt; re-verifying them needs the public UCI files, which requires the owner's approval to download.
